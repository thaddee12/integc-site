Buildings · 27 November 2020

New DGI headquarters inaugurated by the Prime Minister

Prime Minister and Head of Government Joseph DION NGUTE inaugurated the new headquarters of the General Directorate of Taxation (DGI), part of the Ministry of Finance, in Yaoundé on 27 November 2020. This landmark building was constructed under the supervision and control of consulting firm INTEGC: an imposing complex comprising an 11-storey tower and a 4-storey annex.

The facility includes three levels of underground parking with capacity for 1,500 vehicles, a meeting room on every floor, and a large event hall equipped for projection, video-conferencing and simultaneous interpretation. Its hybrid electrical system compensates for power outages and voltage drops. The building also features CCTV surveillance, a 150-seat auditorium, a 100-seat modern restaurant, a library, a gym, a call centre and a state-of-the-art data centre.

Structural Works · 3 May 2018

Rehabilitation of the Bénoué River Bridge

Rehabilitation works on the Bénoué River bridge began on 15 November 2015 and were completed on 3 May 2018, when the structure was handed over by the Ministry of Public Works, the project's client. This 405.60 m long structure features two reinforced concrete girders, each 2.00 m deep, spanning 12 continuous 33.80 m sections; the overall width of the structure is 8 m.

Structural Works · 14 March 2018

New Mayo Galké bridge handed over (national road No. 13)

On 14 March 2018, the new Mayo Galké bridge, located between the towns of Tcholliré and Touboro in the North Region, was handed over by the Ministry of Public Works, the project's client. This 144 m span structure has a 7 m carriageway, two 1.50 m footpaths on either side, and 8 simply-supported 18 m sections made up of 6 precast reinforced concrete T-girders, each 1.34 m deep, working compositely with a 21 cm reinforced concrete deck slab.

Company Life · 1 May 2018

The 132nd Labour Day marked by innovation at INTEGC

The theme of the 132nd International Labour Day — "Social dialogue: a driver of decent work and socio-economic progress in Cameroon" — aligned closely with the company's own aspirations. Two events made 1 May 2018 particularly memorable at INTEGC Sarl: the first-ever address delivered by staff representatives, and the first celebration of the occasion at the new Dragage headquarters.

Considerable space was given to the firm's very first staff representatives, who spoke freely about employee concerns. The new headquarters comprises two buildings of seven and five storeys respectively, built to improve working conditions for all staff.
