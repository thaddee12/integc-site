HOW WE WORK

Two strands, one standard

From design to delivery, our mission is built around two complementary strands: studies, which define the asset, and supervision, which guarantees its proper execution.

STRAND A
Design & Studies

Feasibility studies

Technical, economic and environmental analysis to validate the project's rationale, design options and sizing.

Preliminary & detailed design

Detailed engineering, calculation notes and drawings taken to the level required to launch the tender process.

Structural design & modelling

Structural calculations, hydraulic and geotechnical studies, modelling in line with current standards.

Tender documentation

Drafting the technical and administrative documents required for a clear, fair competitive tender.

STRAND B
Supervision & Control

Quality control & supervision

Ongoing verification that works comply with drawings, standards and technical specifications.

Scheduling, coordination & project management (OPC)

Schedule management, coordination between parties and control of interfaces between trades.

Site supervision

On-site technical presence, site meetings, phase sign-off and management of unforeseen issues.

Handover assistance (AOR)

Follow-up on snagging lists and support for the client through to final handover.
