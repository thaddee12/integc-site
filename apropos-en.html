ABOUT US

A quarter-century building Africa

An independent consulting engineering firm based in Yaoundé, INTEGC brings engineering rigour to major public and private projects in Cameroon and across several African countries, since the year 2000.

OUR ORIGINS

Technical competence as our only signature

Founded in June 2000 as a limited liability company, INTEGC has established itself as a leading consulting engineering firm in civil and infrastructure engineering, recognised by major public clients in Cameroon and across several markets on the African continent.

With a share capital of 300 million FCFA and a multidisciplinary team of more than 110 supervisory staff, the firm covers the full lifecycle of an asset, from initial studies through to the handover of completed works.
